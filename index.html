About

The short version

I started my first company at Bauman Moscow State Technical University. Since then I've founded six companies across Russia, Singapore, Germany, and the US. Two exits: Glance Clock (acquired by NeXtime) and RuJack (acquired by LG Electronics). The pattern is always the same: find a domain where the technology doesn't exist yet, build the first working version, prove it's real.

At Mighty Buildings, I took photopolymer composites from unproven concept to the first UL-listed 3D-printed building elements in the world. Under a year. $20M revenue. Co-authored UL3401 and ISO/ASTM 52939. Through BuildTech Ventures, I now do that across a broader portfolio: spatial computing, carbon tech, AI workflows, logistics.

How I think: Move fast on hard problems. Find customers before you have a product. Standards are competitive advantages, not compliance work. Build the proof, then the company.

Ventures

Companies I've built

Six companies across four countries. Two exits. The common thread: building in domains where the technology doesn't exist yet.

Mighty Buildings

Co-Founder · CIO / COO · 2017 – 2025

3D printing, advanced materials, and robotic automation for construction. Took a materials hypothesis from lab to the first UL-listed 3D-printed building elements in the world. $200M+ raised. 60+ buildings delivered. Co-authored UL3401 and ISO/ASTM 52939. YC W18.

BuildTech Ventures

Managing Partner · 2021 – Present

Silicon Valley investment group backing ConTech and RealTech companies. Portfolio includes Higharc, HyWatts, Beam, Bundle, Tangible Materials, ARX, Wreno, Rem Brain, Kite Compliance, and others. Pre-Seed through Series A.

BuildTech Launchpad

Founder · 2025 – Present

US market growth accelerator for construction and real estate tech startups. We help companies from Europe, the UK, and beyond validate use cases, land pilots, and close early revenue with US corporates. In partnership with Plug and Play.

Echoes of Future Matter

Co-Founder · 2025 – Present

Cultural research laboratory exploring materiality and design futures. Exhibitions, panels, and interviews at the intersection of architecture, design, and emerging technologies. Co-founded with Andrei Hakhovich.

Glance Clock

Co-Founder · COO · 2016 – 2020

Smart wall clock that shows contextual information from your apps. Calendar, weather, calls, voice assistant responses. Built in Singapore, shipped 3,000+ units. Acquired by NeXtime.

SVET Labs

Founder · CEO · 2016 – 2017

The first health-friendly LED light bulb. Mimics daylight dynamics to support circadian rhythm. Rich-spectrum output, built-in biological safety system. Built in Singapore and Moscow.

Trinity Retail Solutions

Co-Founder · CEO · 2005 – 2012

Digital content distribution kiosks for retail. Launched in Moscow, expanded to Germany. Presented at Gamescom 2009. Pioneered on-demand content before streaming took over.

RuJack

Co-Founder · CTO

Hardware technology venture. Acquired by LG Electronics.
