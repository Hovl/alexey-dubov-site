The Pattern

Same playbook, six companies

Start with the hard part

I started at Bauman Moscow State Technical University studying robotics. Since then I've founded six companies across Russia, Singapore, Germany, and the US. Two exits: Glance Clock (acquired by NeXtime) and RuJack (acquired by LG Electronics).

Every time the same move: find a domain where the technology doesn't exist yet, build the first working version, prove it's real. Not the product. The proof.

The Mighty Buildings years

At Mighty Buildings (YC W18), I built the path from photopolymer composites to the first UL-listed 3D-printed building elements in the world. Under a year to first certification. $200M+ raised. 60+ buildings delivered. 20+ patents and inventions. I co-authored UL3401 and ISO/ASTM 52939, the global standards that now govern 3D-printed construction.

The real lesson wasn't about 3D printing. It was about moving fast on hard problems across physics, chemistry, manufacturing, and regulation at the same time.

Now I do it at portfolio scale

BuildTech Ventures backs early-stage teams in the built environment, energy, and robotics. The Launchpad helps European construction tech companies land in the US. Echoes of Future Matter (co-founded with Andrei Hakhovich) is where I work on the cultural side, where architecture, design, and new materials intersect.

How I Think

How I think

Not aspirational. How I actually make decisions.

Face hard problems directly

Don't route around regulatory, technical, or market challenges. Engage them head-on.

"They began by looking at why others in this space had so far failed. They honed in on the fact that printing structures wasn't the main problem, but the regulations. So, instead of dodging this issue, they decided to engage that head on, early on." DealMakers Podcast, 2021

Same statistic, opposite meaning

Surface numbers don't tell the real story. The work is figuring out what the data actually means.

"Construction's low adoption numbers mean low urgency. Energy's low numbers sit on top of 40-year-old grid infrastructure, PLCs nobody can get parts for, and CIOs already budgeting to fix it. Same statistic. Opposite meaning." LinkedIn, Aug 2026

Rethink the process, don't automate it

Biggest wins come from cutting out steps that shouldn't exist, not from speeding up legacy workflows.

"The biggest wins don't come from automating an existing process. They come from rethinking the process from the ground up and cutting out steps that shouldn't exist in the first place." LinkedIn, Jul 2026

Quantify the real constraint

Don't repeat industry consensus. Find the constraint nobody else is pricing in.

"Everyone's writing about power and permitting as the constraints on the AI infrastructure buildout. Both real. But there's a quieter one that doesn't show up until commissioning day, and it's costing people a lot more than a late transformer would." LinkedIn, Aug 2026

Team building is the core skill

Everything starts and ends with the people.

"The number one most critical skill for entrepreneurs is the ability to build a team." DealMakers Podcast, 2021

Standards are competitive advantages

Writing the rules for your industry isn't compliance work. It's shaping the market.

"Standards are competitive advantages, not compliance work. Build the proof, then the company." Personal principle

Standards I co-authored

International Standard

ISO/ASTM 52939:2023

Additive manufacturing for construction — qualification principles for structural and infrastructure elements. The global regulatory backbone for 3D-printed construction. Adopted in 30+ countries.

Industry Standard

UL 3401

Outline of investigation for additive manufacturing in construction. The standard under which Mighty Buildings achieved the first UL-listed 3D-printed building elements in the world.

Academic Publication

Two peer-reviewed papers on polymer composites replacing steel in construction and quality assurance for additive manufacturing in regulated environments.
